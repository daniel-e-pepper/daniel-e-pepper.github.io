This is a set of notes on an accelerated mini course I gave which serves as an introduction to measure theory and integration. The goal is a broad but motivated overview of the elements of Lebesgue's measures and integral. The approach is hopefully human, with lots of motivation and curiosities to explore. Every definition is motivated, rather than simply given. Proofs are relatively scarce; exercises have largely taken their place, as "mathematics is not a spectator sport". Some proofs are given to guide the reader's thinking. If you have any questions on how to fill in any gaps, please feel free to reach out to me, as I'm happy to answer.

For the foreseeable future, these notes will continue to grow and change. You can find the notes in their current form [here](https://daniel-e-pepper.github.io/assets/files/TheoryofMeasuresMC.pdf)
